About this assessment

criticality = hazard_factor + socio_factor. hazard_factor rises once the combined multi-hazard score (landslide × 1.4, river flooding × 0.6, flash-flood catchment × 1.2) crosses a base threshold; socio_factor works the same way for the socioeconomic score (population, land use, network centrality).
